# Today screen — context

**Job:** "What's my run today, and am I ready for it?" Runners open Today once or twice a day, usually minutes before heading out. Today is a wall of equal-weight cards; the brief is to give it a clear hero and room to breathe, and to show a few bolder directions too.
**Mode:** mixed — "clean it up so the run is obvious, but show me a couple of wild ones"
**Flow:** arrives from app launch and the workout reminder notification; leaves to Workout detail (tap the workout), Record (Start run), Plan tab, Progress tab, Shoe detail.

## Content contract
- Greeting + date ("Good morning, Maya" · Saturday, Oct 4)
- Plan context: Week 7 of 16 · Lakeshore Marathon in 63 days
- Today's workout: name, structure (warm-up / 4 × 1.6 km at 4:55/km / cool-down), total 11.2 km, ~58 min, target pace 4:55/km
- Primary action: Start run (the only primary button)
- Readiness: 78% recovered (slept 7 h 40 m, resting HR 52)
- This week: 32.4 of 48 km, 4 of 5 runs done, day dots Mon–Sun (Fri rest, Sat today, Sun long run)
- Weather window: 14° cloudy now; best window 7–9 am, rain after 11
- Streak: 11 days active
- Last run: Thursday, easy 10.2 km, 5:42/km, avg HR 148
- Shoes: Daily trainers at 612 of 700 km, "replace soon"
- Coach note (one line): "Hold the reps honest; the last one should feel like 8/10."

## Neighbours' language
| Screen | Surface | Header | Type (hero/row/caption) | Colour roles | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plan | white raised cards r24 on warm ground | large title "Plan" | title 22 / headline 17 / caption 12 | accent only on "Start"; workout-type chips in metric hues | week list of workouts, one row per day |
| Progress | same cards | large title | display 40 numbers | metric hues: distance blue, pace green, heart red | charts are bars, never rings |
| Record | full-bleed dark | none | display numbers | accent Stop/Pause | map + live pace |
| Club | same cards | large title | headline rows | neutral | feed of friends' runs |

## Rules in force
- One accent (orange #FF5A1F) for actions and the primary element only.
- Metric hues are fixed: distance blue, pace green, heart red, elevation purple, recovery amber.
- Tab-root cards: white, radius 24, soft shadow; no card inside a card.
- Tabs: Today · Plan · Progress · Club, Record as the centre action (5 slots).
- Sentence case everywhere; numbers tabular with smaller units.

## Latitude
- Order, grouping and emphasis of everything on the screen; which items collapse behind a "door".
- Wildcards may drop the card grammar (full-bleed, pager, poster) if they say what else would change.
